# Awesome AI Graded Readers [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of AI tools and resources for creating, finding, and reading graded readers: stories and short books written at a learner's language level.

Graded readers work because the text sits just above what you already know. AI now makes it possible to get a story about the exact topic you care about, in the language you are learning, at roughly the right level. This list covers AI generators, reading apps, open-source readers for your own texts, level checkers, and free leveled reading. AI output still needs a check: ask a teacher or fluent speaker to review vocabulary, grammar, and cultural details before you study from it or share it with a class.

⭐ = recommended pick.

## AI Graded Reader Generators

Tools that write a new story or book at the level and on the topic you choose.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - Writes a complete short book (from about 5,000 words) with a cover on any topic, in the book language you pick; describe the level, vocabulary, and tone in the brief, then export Word or EPUB for your e-reader. Text only, no inside illustrations. $0 7-day trial (card required) includes a first 10,000-word book, then paid.
- [Gradia](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.betapilot.gradia) - Android app that generates CEFR-matched stories with audio and tap-to-translate.
- [LingunAI StorySplice](https://lingunai.com/features/ai-story-generator) - Story generator that works your chosen flashcard words into a 50 to 500 word story at a CEFR level, with a full translation.
- [LitBook](https://litbook.app/) - Short CEFR-matched reading lessons with eight comprehension and grammar questions per passage.
- [NativePal](https://nativepal.app/) - Mobile app that writes stories and lessons about your own topics at CEFR, JLPT, HSK, or TOPIK levels.
- [Outloud Reader](https://outloudreader.app/guides/ai-texts) - AI stories, dialogues, and articles from A1 to C2, read aloud with phrase highlighting.

## Interactive Reading Apps

Leveled or learner-friendly texts with built-in translation and audio.

- [Beelinguapp](https://beelinguapp.com/) - Parallel-text stories and audiobooks shown side by side in two languages.
- [Du Chinese](https://duchinese.net/) - Graded Mandarin stories and articles from newbie to master, with audio and pinyin.
- [LingQ](https://www.lingq.com/) - Reading and listening library that also imports your own texts and tracks known words.
- [Readlang](https://readlang.com/) - Web reader with click-to-translate and automatic flashcards for any text you import.
- [Satori Reader](https://www.satorireader.com/) - Japanese stories written for learners, with audio, explanations, and furigana control.
- [The Chairman's Bao](https://www.thechairmansbao.com/) - Graded Chinese news lessons sorted by HSK level.

## Open-Source Readers for Your Own Texts

Free tools for reading generated or imported books with lookups and vocabulary tracking.

- ⭐ [Lute v3](https://github.com/LuteOrg/lute-v3) - Learning Using Texts: self-hosted reader that tracks the status of every word you meet.
- [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) - Open-source spaced repetition flashcards for the vocabulary you collect while reading.
- [calibre](https://calibre-ebook.com/) - E-book manager and converter for organizing EPUB readers and sending them to devices.
- [KOReader](https://koreader.rocks/) - Open-source e-reader app with dictionary lookups and vocabulary builder.
- [Learning with Texts](https://github.com/HugoFara/learning-with-texts) - Actively maintained app stemmed from the classic LWT for reading foreign texts with word status and lookups.
- [Yomitan](https://github.com/yomidevs/yomitan) - Pop-up dictionary browser extension, popular for reading Japanese and other languages.

## Level and Vocabulary Checkers

Check whether a generated text actually matches the intended level.

- ⭐ [Text Inspector](https://textinspector.com/) - English text analyser with CEFR word levels, lexical diversity, and readability scores.
- [English Profile](https://www.englishprofile.org/) - Research program behind the English Vocabulary Profile, which maps words and meanings to CEFR levels.
- [Lextutor VocabProfile](https://www.lextutor.ca/vp/) - Free tool that splits a text into frequency bands to show how many words are beyond a learner.

## Free Leveled Reading

No-cost texts already written at learner levels.

- [Breaking News English](https://www.breakingnewsenglish.com/) - English news lessons rewritten at several levels with exercises.
- [Global Storybooks](https://globalstorybooks.net/) - Free multilingual children's stories with audio, useful for early learners.
- [Lingua.com](https://lingua.com/) - Free reading texts and exercises in several languages by level.
- [nachrichtenleicht](https://www.nachrichtenleicht.de/) - Weekly news in simple German from Deutschlandfunk.
- [News in Levels](https://www.newsinlevels.com/) - English news articles written at three levels with audio.
- [News in Slow Spanish](https://www.newsinslowspanish.com/) - News and stories in slowed-down Spanish with transcripts.
- [NHK News Web Easy](https://www3.nhk.or.jp/news/easy/) - Japanese news in simplified language with furigana.
- [Tadoku Free Books](https://tadoku.org/japanese/en/free-books-en/) - Free leveled Japanese readers from the NPO Tadoku Supporters.

## Graded Reader Series

Established series that show what good leveled writing looks like.

- [Penguin Readers](https://www.penguinreaders.co.uk/) - English graded readers aligned to CEFR, from starter to advanced.
- [StoryLearning](https://www.storylearning.com/) - Olly Richards' short story books and courses for learning through stories in many languages.
